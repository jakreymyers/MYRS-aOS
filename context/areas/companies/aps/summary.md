# American Physical Society (APS)

Jak's employer — a nonprofit scientific society that publishes ~48,000 manuscripts annually and operates 42 strategic initiatives across five value propositions. Conservative on AI adoption due to staff concerns about environmental impact, ethics, copyright, and job displacement.

## Current

- **SLT (Senior Leadership Team)**: The body of chiefs — CFO/COO, Chief HR Officer, Chief External Affairs Officer, Chief Marketing Officer, Chief Publications Officer, and others
- **CMT (Core Management Team)**: The body of directors and department heads — functional leaders across the organization
- **Departments**: Editorial, sales, EPPMO, international affairs, IS, HR, governance, marketing, finance, membership, publications, strategic communications
- **IS Department**: ~5 core functions (product, engineering, technical ops, customer experience, digital experience); most heavily depended-upon shared service (31 initiative references)
- **Meetings**: All Staff = APS-wide; All Minds = IS department-specific; Annual Leadership Meeting (ALM) = broader leadership event
- **Strategic Context**: 2026 Global Physics Summit (March) is major near-term event; organizational value streams mapped (Kimi, July 2025); RFI/RFP evaluation process established
- **AI Adoption Barriers**: Leadership skepticism about job displacement, environmental sustainability, ethical concerns, copyright risks, and whether AI improves outcomes; tool proliferation creating workforce pain
