# American Physical Society (APS)

Nonprofit scientific society and premier physics publishing organization. Processes ~67,000 manuscripts annually via PRISM (peer review and editorial management system) across 17 journals. 2026 strategic portfolio has 42 initiatives across 5 strategic themes.

## Current

- **SLT (Senior Leadership Team)**: The body of chiefs — CFO/COO, Chief HR Officer, Chief External Affairs Officer, Chief Marketing Officer, Chief Publications Officer, and others
- **CMT (Core Management Team)**: The body of directors and department heads — functional leaders across the organization
- **Departments**: Development, Editorial, Editorial Operations, EPPMO, Ethics, Executive Office, Finance, Global Sales, HR, IS, International Affairs, Marketing, Meetings, Membership, Careers Education & Inclusive Communities, Public Affairs, Publishing, Strategic Communications, Inclusion & Collaboration
- **IS Department**: Most heavily depended-upon shared service (31 initiative references); 5 core functions (product, engineering, technical ops, customer experience, experience design)
- **Meetings**: All Staff = APS-wide; All Minds = IS department-specific; ALM = broader leadership event
- **Meeting Tools**: Fathom is primary transcript intelligence tool; Zoom transcription and AI summarization also used; some meetings documented via shared Google Docs
- **AI Adoption**: Organizational skepticism about job displacement, environmental sustainability, ethical concerns, copyright risks; tool proliferation creating workforce pain
- **Brand**: Brand Blue (#00538B), Brand Teal (#00B4D8), Purple (#1F1C6B), Orange (#FF8F1C); Jost (sans-serif) and Faustina (serif)
