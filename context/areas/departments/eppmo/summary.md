# EPPMO — Enterprise Portfolio & Project Management Office

EPPMO is APS's strategic portfolio governance office led by Dona Williams, responsible for managing the enterprise portfolio through Lean Charter analysis and audience-specific governance decks. They maintain the authoritative portfolio tracker in Airtable and produce five tailored review decks (CEO/SLT, CMT Directors, Initiative Leads, Internal, Shared Services) that drive strategic decision-making across the organization.
