# Finance & Reporting Department

The Finance & Reporting Department is APS's financial hub, led by Director Sandy Weldon with 13 staff members. They provide timely and accurate financial information to support organizational decision-making and strategic initiatives.

## Current

- **Leadership**: Sandy Weldon (Director) with direct reports Maria Inch (Office & Payroll Manager), Melaku Denbel (Finance Business Analyst), and Lakesia Brownlee (Controller)
- **Team Structure**: Controller oversees Grants Team (Kareema Sesay, Miaya Milligan), Publishing Accounting (Janelle Tupper), General Services Accounting (Nichole Davis, Pahola Elder, Dana Alford-Armstrong, Janet Harris, Abtahee Rahman), and Senior Accountant (Marcia Johnson)
- **Core Responsibilities**: Corporate credit card/expense/vendor management, revenue recognition, annual financial audit and budget, tax and regulatory compliance, financial reporting and analytics
- **Strategic Projects**: (1) Implement 3-year rolling business forecast, (2) Vet possible new accounting system — both aligned to Pillar 4 (Organizational Effectiveness)
- **Key Collaborations**: Works with Publication Sales on Salesforce CPQ for institutional sales recording, invoicing, monitoring, and sales tax compliance
