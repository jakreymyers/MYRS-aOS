# publications

The Publications department manages APS's manuscript processing and peer review operations across 17 journals, handling 48K manuscripts annually. The PWT initiative is a 2-year transformation effort launched with an RFI in February 2026 to modernize desk triage, review coordination, and production workflows.

## Current

- APS Publications Workflow Transformation (PWT) is a 2-year end-to-end initiative with RFI issued 2026-02-04
- Scope includes manuscript desk triage, peer review coordination, and production pipeline
- Handles 48K manuscripts/year across 17 journals
