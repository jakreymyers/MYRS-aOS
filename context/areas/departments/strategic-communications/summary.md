# Strategic Communications (StratComms) Department

APS department advancing organizational goals through integrated storytelling and trusted engagement across multiple channels and audiences.

- **Functional areas**: Society Communications, Science Communications, Internal Communications, APS News, Email, Social Media, Leadership Communications & Reputation
- **Key audiences**: Members, scientists, policymakers, public, and staff
