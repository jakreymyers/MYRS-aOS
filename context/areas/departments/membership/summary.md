# Membership

APS maintains a formal membership engagement strategy centered on regular surveys and data-driven initiatives. The organization conducts annual membership surveys with unit-level analysis and is actively exploring member data use cases through the Actable Discovery project.

## Current
- Conducts formal Membership Survey with unit-level highlights analysis (2024 data: 2.8 MB Excel workbook)
- Exploring member data use cases through Actable Discovery project with stakeholder interviews underway (February 2026)
- Maintains donor cultivation and stewardship processes alongside survey-driven engagement strategy
- Data platform evaluation in progress to support member insights
