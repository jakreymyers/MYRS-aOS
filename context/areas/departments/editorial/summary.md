# Editorial

APS Editorial department led by Jessica Thomas. Responsible for 17 world-leading physics journals and Physics Magazine, with approximately 70-80 staff.

## Current
- Led by Jessica Thomas
- ~70-80 direct staff members
- Structure: Associate Executive Editors oversee journal portfolios by theme; each journal has a Chief Editor leading 4-20 Associate Editors
- Key responsibilities: peer review for significance/rigor/relevance, research trend tracking, reviewer pool cultivation, ethics and research integrity, making physics visible
- Current topical groups: astronomy/astrophysics, biological physics, climate/sustainability, chemical physics, experimental condensed matter physics
