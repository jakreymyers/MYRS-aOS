# Editorial Department

The Editorial Department, led by Jessica Thomas, is responsible for 17 physics journals and Physics Magazine. Approximately 70-80 direct staff.

## Current

- Led by Jessica Thomas; Daniel Ucko (Head of Ethics and Research Integrity), Jennifer Tommolino (Executive Assistant)
- ~70-80 direct staff
- Organizational structure: Associate Executive Editors oversee journal portfolios by theme; Chief Editors lead 4-20 Associate Editors per journal; Managing and Assistant Editors support external teams
- Core responsibilities: manuscript evaluation (peer review), research trend monitoring, reviewer pool cultivation, ethics and research integrity oversight, physics visibility and engagement
- Active topical focus areas: astronomy/astrophysics, biological physics, climate/sustainability, chemical physics, experimental condensed matter physics
- Currently piloting AI policy for editorial operations
