# International Affairs (INTAF) Department

INTAF drives APS's global physics community engagement and international partnerships under Director Amy Flatten. The department focuses on establishing APS as a welcoming global hub and expanding collaboration with international physics organizations.

## Current

- **Leadership**: Amy Flatten (Director) → Francis Slakey (Chief External Affairs Officer, SLT)
- **Team**: 5 members including Michele Irwin (Senior International Programs Manager), Shanaz Sulejmanovic, and Lais Ribeiro
- **Mission**: Convene global physics community, establish APS as welcoming global hub, expand partnerships with national/international physics societies
- **2025 Global Physics Summit**: Managed 17 satellite sites, 11 contributed sessions, 6 watch parties
- **Ukraine Support Program**: Sloan Foundation grant enabled 15 physicists to collaborate with US/Europe partners; 5 grad students/postdocs attended international conferences; 3 physicists relocated to Italy
- **African Physics Newsletter**: 1,226 subscribers across 40 African countries + diaspora, 46.2% open rate (highest of all APS newsletters, Nov 2024)
- **Policy Concern**: Monitoring US administration impacts on international scientific collaboration, visa issues, and restrictions on scientist visits/collaboration
