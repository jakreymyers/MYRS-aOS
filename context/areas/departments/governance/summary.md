# Governance Department

Compact 2-person unit led by Jeanette Russo (APS Corporate Secretary) reporting to CEO Jonathan Bagger. Provides strategic governance oversight across the entire APS organization with wide-reaching influence.

## Current

- **Leadership & Structure**: Jeanette Russo (Corporate Secretary) and Vicki Scudlark (Corporate Governance Associate); reports to Executive Office
- **Core Responsibilities**: Strengthen governance framework, support volunteer leadership, foster collaboration on APS mission, cultivate diverse leadership pipeline, advance good governance practices
- **Elections & Nominations**: Manages APS General Election (VP, General Councilor, International Councilor, Chair-Elect of Nominating Committee) and year-round committee nomination process
- **Active Initiative**: APS Units Task Force to realign unit governance and operations—identifying improvements, clarifying roles, addressing risks, achieving governance consistency
